For each delta above, one line of rationale (WHY it matters, what it costs, expected impact):

- **#1** — Gap-ID drift between the orchestrator's backlog labels and a block file's internal IDs caused two mid-wave corrections and one state-file rename (B65-G3→B65-G1). Cheap to prevent (one instruction line); impact is cleaner cross-references and fewer integration reconciliations.
- **#2** — Six of fourteen writers spent tokens rediscovering and re-explaining the same benign tool artifact. A kit note turns 6 rediscoveries into 1 citation.
- **#3** — Standardizes the strongest form of a negative claim (whole-artifact read) vs the weakest (one grep), which the corpus already prefers in practice.
